# LTX speed per GPU (2026-10-01)

Trial (random) weights, same speed as the real model. Denoise: `scripts/benchmark_ltx_denoise.py` (3 windows,
median of 3). Decode: `scripts/benchmark_ltx_decode.py` (one 121-frame window). The 5090 rows were measured on the
local card at the commits in the CSV; 5080, 4090 and 3090 on rented cards at `2e67910`; the 2080 Ti on a rented card
running the `perf/ltx-sm75` work (merged in `93bfba7`). CSV: `2026-10-01_ltx_gpus.csv`.

## Per window at 512 px, distilled INT8 (8 steps)

| GPU (VRAM) | denoise ms/step | decode s/window (segment / whole video) | ≈ s per window | blocks on GPU |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RTX 5090 (32 GB) | 491 | 1.12 / 0.86 | 4.8 | 48/48 |
| RTX 5080 (16 GB) | 962 | 2.25 / 1.75 | 9.5 | 44/48 |
| RTX 4090 (24 GB) | 655 | 1.71 / 1.33 | 6.6 | 48/48 |
| RTX 3090 (24 GB) | 1541 | 3.30 / 2.55 | 15 | 48/48 |
| RTX 2080 Ti (11 GB) | 3137 | 22.6 / 17.7 (8 GiB) | 43–48 | 25/48 |

≈ s per window = 8 × denoise step + decode at the whole-video budget (2080 Ti: both budgets).

## End to end, 30 s 1080p30 clip, `--ltx-trial` (5090, `2e67910`)
Distilled INT8 147 s (4.9× realtime), NVFP4 96 s (3.2×), undistilled 205 s (6.8×).

## Notes
- 16 GB cards stream a few INT8 blocks from RAM at almost no cost; 11 GB streams half of them.
- The rented 5080 ran at a 300 W cap (stock 360 W), the 3090 at its 350 W cap, the 2080 Ti locked at 1350 MHz:
  stock cards are a few % (2080 Ti 15–30 %) faster.
- RTX 20 (SM 7.5): before `perf/ltx-sm75`, LTX crashed in the denoise (no bf16 attention kernel below SM 8.0) and a
  decode took 317 s. Triton has no tensor-core math below SM 8.0 and Inductor skips bf16 graphs there, so these cards
  run the INT8 GEMMs on cuBLAS and neighborhood attention in fp16 without compile. An fp16 decoder would roughly halve
  their decode (22.5 → 13.1 s in a test); not done.
